Our Journey

MIT Computer Training Center began in 1999 as a small initiative to bring ICT education to Bopitiya.

MIT became one of the pioneers introducing structured IT education to the local community.

In 2001, the institute was officially registered as a sole proprietorship business.

In 2014, MIT was registered under TVEC, and in 2020, the institute achieved accreditation to conduct NVQ courses.

Today, MIT stands as a leading ICT training institute, empowering students through practical, industry-oriented education.
